# LEAKLEVEL – Smart Floor-Wise Water Leakage Detection System

**Intelligent Floor-Wise Water Leakage Detection System** – a software-only hackathon prototype.

## What LEAKLEVEL does

In apartment and commercial buildings, many floors share one water pipeline network, so when a leak happens it is hard to tell *which floor* it is on. LEAKLEVEL watches the **water flow** and **pipeline pressure** on each floor's branch line, detects abnormal combinations, identifies the **suspected floor**, and notifies maintenance staff.

This prototype **simulates** the sensors in the browser. No hardware is needed.

## Data sources (transparency)

Every panel is labelled with where its numbers come from:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Seeded historical data** (purple) | Preloaded sample records generated by `data.js` with a fixed seed. Identical on every run. Illustrative only. |
| **Simulated live data** (cyan) | Generated every few seconds by the JavaScript simulation in `script.js`. |
| **Real sensor data** (grey) | Measurements from installed hardware. **Not connected** in this prototype. |

Seeded or simulated readings are never presented as actual building measurements.

### Seeded baselines (illustrative, not measured)

| Floor | Normal Flow (L/min) | Normal Pressure (bar) |
|-------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | 18.5 | 2.8 |
| 2 | 22.0 | 2.6 |
| 3 | 16.5 | 2.9 |
| 4 | 20.0 | 2.7 |
| 5 | 14.0 | 3.0 |

Normal flow is the peak-hour reference. Historical hourly readings follow a typical residential demand pattern (morning and evening peaks, low at night). Real baselines must be calibrated using installed sensors.

### What the seed dataset contains

- **Baseline data**: normal flow and pressure per floor, expected daily usage.
- **Historical data**: hourly readings for the past 7 days for all 5 floors, plus 5-minute readings around each incident (about 1,000 records in total).
- **5 past incidents**: 3 leaks (Floor 2 at night, Floor 3 in the morning peak, Floor 1 at night), 1 running cistern (high flow only), 1 pump-servicing pressure dip (false alarm).
- **Maintenance records**: detection, acknowledgement and resolution times, team, action taken and outcome.
- **Sensor inventory**: 10 sensors with signal, 7-day uptime and calibration dates (sample metadata).

The seeded statuses are produced by the same detection rules the live dashboard uses. You can download the full dataset from **Records → Historical Sensor Records → Download CSV**.

To use real meter data later, replace the generated `hourly`, `detail` and `incidents` arrays in `data.js` with records loaded from hardware.

## How to run

1. Open the `Leaklevel` folder.
2. Double-click `index.html`. It opens in your web browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).

That's it. No installation needed.

## How to present with Demo Mode

At the top of the Dashboard, use the **Demo Mode** buttons:

1. **Normal Building**: all five floors normal, no alerts.
2. **Single Floor Leakage**: Floor 3's flow rises and pressure drops. It shows WARNING ("confirming 1/3, 2/3") and then turns **LEAK SUSPECTED** after 3 readings (about 6 seconds). A red banner appears, the Floor 3 card turns red, the alert is saved in Alert History, and a task appears in the Maintenance Panel.
3. **Multiple Floor Abnormality**: Floor 2 (major) and Floor 4 (moderate) both become suspected floors.
4. **Hidden Riser Leak**: every floor looks normal, but the main meter shows more water entering than all floors use. The Water Balance Check confirms a riser leak and estimates its position (between Floor 3 and Floor 4).
5. **Pump Failure**: pressure drops on every floor at once with no extra flow. LEAKLEVEL diagnoses a **supply / pump issue** instead of raising five false leak alarms.

With **Auto shut-off valve** turned on (default), a confirmed floor leak closes that floor's valve after 4 leak readings. The floor turns purple (**VALVE CLOSED**), the banner says **LEAK CONTAINED**, and the estimated water saved is shown. "Mark as Inspected" reopens the valve.

**Reset Building** restores all default readings.

Suggested story for judges:
**Normal building → Simulate leak → Floor 3 identified → Valve closes automatically → SMS preview sent → Maintenance marks as inspected → Show hidden riser leak and pump failure (what simple sensors miss).**

## Smart features

| Feature | How it works |
|---------|--------------|
| Water Balance Check | Main meter (inlet) vs sum of 5 floor meters. Unaccounted water above 2 L/min or 5% for 3 readings means a riser leak is suspected. Floors with a pressure deficit above 5% suggest where it is. |
| Pump vs Leak | If 3 or more floors lose pressure together and no floor's flow rises, it is a supply issue, not a leak. |
| Auto Shut-off Valve | Simulated motorised valve closes a floor after a confirmed leak. Water saved = excess flow × average manual resolution time from the seeded history. Can be turned off in Detection Settings. |
| Self-Learning Baseline | Learns normal flow for each floor at each hour from the 7-day history. A replay shows it catches more incident hours than a fixed threshold, with no extra false alarms. |
| Night-Flow Leak Check | Average flow between 02:00 and 04:00 per floor per night. Red if more than 2× the floor's typical night flow. |
| Leak Risk Ranking | Score out of 100 from past leaks, warnings, abnormal nights, live alerts and usage above expected. Used to plan preventive inspections. |
| Cost & Environmental Impact | Converts lost water to ₹, kWh and CO₂. Tariffs are editable (default ₹50/kL, ₹8/kWh; 0.3 kWh/kL, 0.71 kg CO₂/kWh). Illustrative estimates only. |
| Alert Notifications | Phone-style preview of SMS / WhatsApp messages to maintenance and residents, with a "Reply ACK" button. **No real messages are sent.** |

## Detection logic (rule-based)

Each floor has a normal **baseline** flow and pressure.

- **Flow abnormal**: flow is more than **30%** above baseline.
- **Pressure abnormal**: pressure is more than **20%** below baseline.

| Status | Rule |
|--------|------|
| NORMAL | Both readings within limits |
| WARNING | One indicator abnormal, or both abnormal but not yet confirmed |
| LEAK SUSPECTED | Both abnormal for **3 consecutive readings** |

Requiring several consecutive readings avoids false alarms from one odd reading. All thresholds can be changed in **Live Monitoring → Detection Settings**.

> These are demonstration thresholds, not validated real-world engineering limits. LEAKLEVEL identifies a *suspected* floor or pipeline branch; physical inspection is needed to confirm the actual leak.

## Other controls

- **Pause / Resume** and **Reset** the simulation.
- **Trigger Leak / Stop Leak** on any floor (Major or Moderate).
- **Manual Sensor Test**: type your own flow and pressure values for a floor.
- **Maintenance Panel**: Acknowledge Alert, Mark as Inspected (simulated repair).
- **Alert History**: log of live-session alerts with a Clear History button (asks for confirmation).
- **Toggle Riser Leak / Toggle Pump Fault**: start or stop a hidden riser leak or a pump fault manually.
- **Toggle Sensor Link**: simulate a floor's sensor node going offline. Detection pauses for that floor and Sensor Health shows it as offline.
- **Analytics → Historical**: 24-hour usage vs expected (choose a day), 7-day daily consumption, floor comparison with leak losses.
- **Records**: baseline data, leakage history, incident replay (5-minute readings), maintenance records, sensor health, filterable historical records and CSV download.

## What is simulated or seeded

- All live flow and pressure readings (generated by JavaScript with small random fluctuations).
- All historical records, incidents, maintenance records and sensor metadata (seeded sample data).
- Sensor "online/offline" status.
- Water consumption and water loss figures (1 reading = 1 simulated minute). These are **estimates, not measured savings**.
- Maintenance acknowledgement and inspection actions.
- The main water meter, the shut-off valves, the pump and the SMS / WhatsApp messages.
- Cost, energy and CO₂ figures (based on illustrative tariffs and factors).
